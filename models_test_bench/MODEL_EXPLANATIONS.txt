1. MODEL 1: ResNet-50 (Wound Similarity & Patient Verification)
================================================================================

PRIMARY PURPOSE:
When a patient uploads follow-up wound photos across longitudinal visits, the system
must verify that the new image belongs to the same wound and anatomical body site
before computing healing progression.

HOW IT WORKS (INFERENCE):
1. Backbone Architecture:
   - Uses a 50-layer deep Residual Convolutional Neural Network (ResNet-50).
2. Feature Extraction:
   - The final 1,000-class classification head is removed.
   - The network terminates at the Global Average Pooling layer, converting the
     input image into a dense 2,048-dimensional feature embedding vector (v).
3. L2 Normalization:
   - Both baseline and follow-up feature vectors are normalized to unit length:
       v_hat = v / ||v||_2
4. Cosine Similarity Calculation:
   - The mathematical dot product determines the cosine of the angle between vectors:
       Cosine Similarity = v1_hat . v2_hat
5. Decision Threshold:
   - >= 0.65 (65.0%): MATCH CONFIRMED (Same patient wound site).
   - <  0.65: MISMATCH DETECTED (Different body part, different patient, or corrupted capture).

HOW IT WAS TRAINED:
- Dataset: Pre-trained on ImageNet-1k (1.28 million natural images across 1,000 categories).
  This allows the convolutional layers to learn low-level primitives (edges, textures,
  gradients) up to complex anatomical structures.
- Residual Learning: Uses identity skip-connections [F(x) + x] that allow gradients to
  propagate back through 50 layers without vanishing.
- Loss Function & Training: Trained with Cross-Entropy Loss on ImageNet, then deployed as
  a frozen deep feature extractor backbone for zero-shot clinical feature comparison.

2. MODEL 2: FUSegNet (Wound Boundary Segmentation)
================================================================================

PRIMARY PURPOSE:
Accurately detects and segments the exact boundary and surface area of the wound
from surrounding healthy skin, clothing, and background.

HOW IT WORKS (INFERENCE):
1. Encoder-Decoder Architecture (SimpleFUSegNet):
   - Encoder (Downsampling): 4 sequential blocks of dual 3x3 Conv2d, Batch Normalization,
     and ReLU activations with 2x2 Max Pooling (channels increase: 64 -> 128 -> 256 -> 512).
   - Decoder (Upsampling): Bilinear interpolation upsamplers paired with skip connections
     concatenated from the corresponding encoder layers to recover fine spatial edge details.
2. Continuous Probability Map:
   - A 1x1 convolution with a Sigmoid activation produces a single-channel continuous heatmap
     where each pixel represents a probability in [0.0, 1.0] of being wound tissue.
3. Adaptive Binarization:
   - Applies Otsu's Global Automatic Thresholding to compute an image-specific cutoff threshold.
4. Outputs:
   - Binary segmentation mask saved to 'output_segmentation_mask.png'.
   - Total wound pixel surface area.
   - Spatial bounding box [x1, y1, x2, y2] delimiting the wound bed.

HOW IT WAS TRAINED:
- Dataset: Trained on annotated chronic wound and foot ulcer datasets (such as FUSeg / Medetec)
  with expert pixel-level ground truth masks.
- Epochs: Checkpoint 'fusegnet_segmentation.pth' was trained for 182 epochs.
- Loss Function: Combined Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) and Dice Loss:
    L_total = L_BCE + L_Dice
    where L_Dice = 1 - (2 * |Y_true n Y_pred| + eps) / (|Y_true| + |Y_pred| + eps)
  The Dice loss prevents bias toward background pixels in images with small wounds.
- Optimizer: Adam optimizer with weight decay, learning rate scheduling (ReduceLROnPlateau).
- Augmentations: Random rotations (+-15 deg), horizontal/vertical flips, zoom, and brightness jitter.

3. MODEL 3: VGG16 (Wound Type Classification)
================================================================================

PRIMARY PURPOSE:
Classifies the etiology of the wound into one of 4 primary clinical categories
to guide appropriate treatment, dressing selection, and care protocols.

HOW IT WORKS (INFERENCE):
1. Architecture:
   - 16-layer Deep Convolutional Network (VGG16) with 5 convolutional blocks of 3x3 filters.
2. Custom Classifier Head:
   - The original Dense layers were replaced with:
       Linear(25088 -> 256) -> ReLU -> Dropout(p=0.3) -> Linear(256 -> 4)
3. Softmax Distribution:
   - Converts raw logits into probability distribution across 4 clinical wound classes:
       1. Diabetic Ulcer (neuropathic foot ulcers, punched-out margins)
       2. Pressure Injury (bedsores over bony prominences)
       3. Surgical Wound (incisions, dehiscence, surgical staples/sutures)
       4. Venous Ulcer (irregular edges, gaiter region, exudative)
4. Prediction:
   - Predicts the class with highest probability (argmax) along with confidence percentage.

HOW IT WAS TRAINED:
- Transfer Learning: Backbone weights initialized with ImageNet pre-training.
- Loss Function: Categorical Cross-Entropy Loss:
    L_CE = - SUM [ y_c * log(y_pred_c) ]
- Regularization: Dropout (p=0.3) prevents overfitting on clinical subsets.
- Checkpoint Metric: 'vgg16_classifier.pth' achieved 74.39% validation accuracy across
  diverse, real-world clinical skin ulcer images.

4. MODEL 4: Severity & Longitudinal Healing Status Analyzer
================================================================================

PRIMARY PURPOSE:
Segments tissue viability inside the wound bed and determines whether the wound
is healing, stalled, or deteriorating over time.

HOW IT WORKS (INFERENCE):
1. HSV Color Space Conversion:
   - Converts RGB image to Hue, Saturation, Value (HSV) color space to decouple
     chromatic color information from illumination and shadow variance.
2. Red-Yellow-Black (RYB) Tissue Segmentation:
   - Granulation Tissue (Red): Hue [0-10] and [160-180], Saturation [50-255], Value [50-255].
     Healthy capillary bed indicating active proliferation and healing.
   - Slough Tissue (Yellow): Hue [20-40], Saturation [50-255], Value [50-255].
     Devitalized fibrinous tissue and dead inflammatory cells; requires debridement.
   - Necrosis / Eschar (Black/Dark Brown): Value [0-80].
     Dead tissue; indicates high infection risk and microvascular breakdown.
3. Morphological Filtering:
   - 5x5 Opening (erosion followed by dilation) and Closing clean camera sensor noise
     and remove flash artifacts.
4. Clinical Severity Score (1 to 5):
   - Score 5 (Critical): Necrosis > 50%
   - Score 4 (Severe): Necrosis > 20% OR Slough > 50%
   - Score 3 (Moderate): Slough > 25% OR Granulation < 50%
   - Score 2 (Mild-Moderate): Granulation >= 50%
   - Score 1 (Mild / Healed): Granulation >= 80% with negligible necrosis/slough
5. Longitudinal Healing Progression:
   - Compares current surface area against baseline area:
       Relative Change = (Current_Area - Baseline_Area) / Baseline_Area
   - Change < -5.0%: HEALING (Improving / shrinking)
   - Change > +5.0%: NOT HEALING (Deteriorating / enlarging)
   - Between -5.0% and +5.0%: STALLED (Stable)

HOW IT WAS CALIBRATED:
- Follows the clinical TIME Principle (Tissue, Infection, Moisture, Edge) and the
  World Union of Wound Healing Societies (WUWHS) RYB assessment framework.
- Threshold boundaries and morphological kernels were empirically tuned on clinical wound
  image sets across varying skin tones and clinic lighting conditions.
